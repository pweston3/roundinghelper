How a question works

  1. Tap the digit in the named place
  2. Name the two round numbers it falls between
  3. Tap the digit just to its right — the one that decides
  4. Choose which neighbor it is closer to

The number line stays hidden until step 2, so the choice comes from reasoning about distance rather than reading the answer off the screen. The digit rule arrives last, as a shortcut.
